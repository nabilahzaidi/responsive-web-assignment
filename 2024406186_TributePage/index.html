Tun Dr. Mahathir Mohamad

The Father of Modernization in Malaysia

Timeline of His Life:

  • 1925 - Born in Alor Setar, Kedah.
  • 1981 - Appointed as the 4th Prime Minister of Malaysia, initiating the era of industrial modernization.
  • 1991 - Launched Vision 2020 (Wawasan 2020) to transform Malaysia into a developed nation.
  • 1996 - Launched the Multimedia Super Corridor (MSC) to drive information technology forward.
  • 2003 - Retired after 22 years of governing and developing the nation's infrastructure.
  • 2018 - Returned as the 7th Prime Minister to continue the development agenda.

"To be a great leader, one must first be a good follower."

-- Tun Dr. Mahathir Mohamad
